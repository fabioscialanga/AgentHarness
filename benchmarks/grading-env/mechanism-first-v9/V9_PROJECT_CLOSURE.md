# Mechanism-first efficacy campaign: final closure

## Terminal result

V9 ended with the preregistered `CEILING` decision after all three calibration anchors completed. The protocol correctly stopped before paired evaluation, with zero evaluation calls.

This result is valid but does not estimate efficacy. It is neither a GO nor a NO-GO for AgentHarness: the baseline was already strong enough on the calibration roster that the benchmark could not support the intended incremental comparison.

## Defensible conclusion

The controlled synthetic repair campaign is closed. Its lineage produced useful engineering evidence about runner integrity, provider accounting, immutable treatment delivery, source-native defects, and fail-closed finalization, but it did not produce evidence that supports a public claim of incremental AgentHarness efficacy.

The correct statement is:

> Under the fixed model, provider, task family, and repair budget studied, the available qualified synthetic tasks did not provide a stable discriminating regime for estimating AgentHarness's incremental repair efficacy.

## What not to claim

- Do not claim that AgentHarness improved repair success from V9.
- Do not treat CEILING as a positive efficacy result.
- Do not treat V7 or V8 receipt failures as negative efficacy results; those runs were invalid before evaluation.
- Do not launch another post-hoc synthetic roster revision to seek a favorable result.

## If evidence work resumes

Use naturally occurring repositories or prospective user studies with independently sampled failures, preregistered endpoints, and a task distribution not authored or selected in response to prior campaign outcomes.
